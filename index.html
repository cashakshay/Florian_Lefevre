I

Ash

Every piece starts as something discarded. Offcut leather, spent sterling, dead-stock twill: we collect what other ateliers throw away and sort it by hand.

II

Melt

Silver goes into a small crucible and melts at 962 °C. Leather soaks in vegetable tannin for three weeks. Nothing here is rushed.

III

Cast

The molten sterling is poured into the broken halo, our house sigil. It becomes every buckle, zip pull, ring and pendant we make.

IV

Cut

Patterns are cut one at a time on a single table. A Lefèvre biker jacket takes one cutter and two sewers eleven days to finish.

V

Relic

Then we age it: sanded, washed, worn in, tarnished. What arrives at your door already looks like it has a past.

Every piece is numbered and signed.
